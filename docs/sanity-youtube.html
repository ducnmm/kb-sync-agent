Query: How do I add a YouTube video?

• Log In: Access the OptiSigns portal and ensure your screens are set up and paired.
• Navigate to Apps: Go to Files/Assets and click on Apps.
• Select YouTube: Search for and click on the YouTube app.
• Enter Video Information: URL: Paste the YouTube video link (regular, share, or Shorts link).
• Preview and Save: The video will play in the preview pane. Click Save to add it to your assets.
