Query: How do I add a YouTube video?

• Log in, open Files/Assets, and click Apps.
• Search for YouTube and open the YouTube app.
• Paste the video link. A name is optional. Regular, share, and Shorts links all work.
• Check the preview, then click Save.
• Put the saved item on a screen, or in a playlist.
